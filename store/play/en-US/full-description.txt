MyOrdbok is a free English–Myanmar dictionary. Look up a word in English or in Myanmar and read its meanings, with examples of use and its parts of speech.

WHAT IS IN IT
• English → Myanmar and Myanmar → English definitions, with examples
• Parts of speech, word forms (went → go) and word families
• Synonyms, opposites and related words
• Pronunciation: hear a word said aloud
• Suggestions as you type, and your recent searches
• Numbers written out in Myanmar and English words
• Grammar notes for learners
• A word of the day

LEARN AND KEEP
• Liked: keep the words you want to come back to
• Practice: short rounds that bring each word back just before it is forgotten, with a daily streak

ANYWHERE
• Offline: download the dictionary and look words up without a connection
• Light and dark themes, phone and tablet layouts
• Myanmar text in Unicode; a word typed in Zawgyi is still found

FREE, WITH NO ADS
Everything in MyOrdbok is free for everyone, with no ads and no account needed to read. Signing in with Google, GitHub or Apple is optional: to comment, suggest a correction, or keep Practice progress across devices.

If MyOrdbok helps you, you can support it with a one-time Supporter purchase or a tip. They unlock nothing; they help keep the dictionary growing.
